About Our Restaurant

Golden Spoon Restaurant is a family-owned dining experience offering classic comfort food made fresh every day. We focus on quality ingredients, friendly service, and a welcoming atmosphere for everyone.

Whether you are stopping in for a quick lunch or enjoying dinner with friends, we strive to make every visit special. Our founder, Toni, has been alive since 1602. This is her fourth restaurant, after the last three tragically burned down.
